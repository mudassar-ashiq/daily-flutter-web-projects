A small bakery, run by the same family since 2012

Fresh Oven started as one oven and a counter on College Road. Fourteen years later the oven is bigger, but the routine is the same: dough mixed at dawn, first trays out by eight, and the shop closed when the day's baking is sold.

We are not a chain and we do not want to be one. If something is not right, tell us at the counter and we will fix it — usually by handing you a fresh one.
